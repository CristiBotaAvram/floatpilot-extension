Is this safe to use with CSFloat?
FloatPilot runs in your browser and adds UI on top of CSFloat pages; it doesn’t automate purchases or send actions to CSFloat. Always follow CSFloat’s Terms.
What data do you collect?
Sign‑in via Steam (OpenID) gives us your SteamID and display name. We also store plan status and purchase history. No marketplace API keys are required or stored.
Does it work on other browsers?
Yes! FloatPilot works on Chromium‑based browsers (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera). Install from the Chrome Web Store; Edge users can also allow extensions from other stores.
Why are there multiple options for purchasing?
We offer different time‑based access options (1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month) so you can choose what fits your trading needs best. Each purchase grants access for the chosen duration; access ends automatically when the time is up.
